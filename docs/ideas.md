# Shortlisted product ideas

Saved 2026-10-19 so later sessions can pick these up. Each Claude session starts fresh, so this file is the memory.

## Founder context
- Principal software engineer, 13+ years: .NET, SQL, AWS, Azure. Building this as a side project alongside a full-time job.
- Can invest some money. Wants revenue early. Complexity in the build is fine.
- Target buyers: consumers (not companies) in the US and UK, worldwide welcome. People who can actually pay.
- Purchase must be a single person deciding and paying in a few minutes (no second party, no training others).
- Areas of interest: health, wealth, relationships, calm and peace.
- Ideas already rejected, and why:
  - visa paperwork kit (needs a human expert)
  - career-break planner (small market, low willingness to pay)
  - Indian freelancer tax tool (users won't share financial data)
  - B2B tools: Fabric cost control, AI spend limits, .NET migration (wants consumers)
  - family scam protection (two parties plus training the parents)

## Idea 1 (top pick): "No contact" breakup recovery coach
- **The moment:** someone just went through a breakup and wants to text their ex at 1 a.m. They buy on impulse, alone.
- **The hook:** "Write it here, not to them." The user writes the message to their ex; the AI responds kindly and starts a 24-hour cooldown.
- **Features:** a shareable no-contact day counter, an urge SOS button, AI journaling, a 30-day recovery plan, and optional night-time blocking of the ex's chat app (Apple Screen Time API).
- **Price:** about $4.99/week, $9.99/month or $39/year. Audience: 18–35, US and UK. iOS first.
- **Competition:** Mend (audio program), ExDetox (no-contact tracking plus AI; reported revenue inconsistent), Breakup Boss (2017). No clear leader.
- **Risks:** people heal and cancel, so price for short high-value use; position as coaching, never therapy.
- **Later:** a "ready to date again" stage.

## Idea 2: Phone-to-bed lock (calm plus sleep)
- **The product:** social apps lock at bedtime until you've slept; sleep improvement is measured from Apple Health.
- **Proof of demand:** Opal (screen-time app) reached $10M ARR bootstrapped with 1M+ daily users, two-thirds of them students. Sleep is the #1 mental-health worry.
- **Competition:** Opal, Time2Sleep, SleepLock, Don't Scroll, HyperSleep. Crowded, so it's a marketing fight.

## Reserve idea: private on-device AI journal
- Not validated: competitors such as Rosebud and Day One haven't been researched yet.

## Key insight
For impulse-buy consumer apps, success is roughly 80% distribution (TikTok/Reels content, either posted yourself or from paid creators at about $50–200 per video) and 20% code.

## Validation plan (about 2 weeks, before building)
1. Create a "no contact / healing" TikTok and Instagram account and post 15–20 short videos.
2. Link to a waitlist page with a price.
3. Build if 1–2 videos pass about 50k views and a few hundred people join the waitlist. The first version takes about 4–6 weeks.
